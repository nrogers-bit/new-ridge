Who we are

New Ridge was founded on 6 September 2026 as a fresh start, and its constitution was ratified the same day. Its people are called Ridgeans. Its culture and symbols are loosely based on the United States, and it hopes to grow a separate national identity of its own.

Government

Congress

Two chambers

The Council of Representatives and the Delegates-Assembly make our laws.

Our provinces

New Ridge is divided into four provinces: Berchester, New Philadelphia, Northampton and Juneau.
